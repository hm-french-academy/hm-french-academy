# Grade 8 annual revision — production question bank v1

This bank is intentionally prerequisite-focused. Items are distributed across all six annual units.

## Diagnostic bank — 36 items

### Identity / Unit 1
1. Choose the correct response to “Comment tu t'appelles ?”
2. Complete: Je ___ Adam. (m'appelle / t'appelles / s'appelle)
3. Match Bonjour / Bonsoir / Au revoir with the appropriate situation.
4. Write numbers 0, 7, 12, 18, 20 in French.
5. Complete: Il est ___ heures.
6. Choose the correct subject pronoun for a named boy.

### Age / Unit 2
7. Complete: J'___ 13 ans.
8. Ask someone’s age using “Quel âge…?”
9. Choose the correct article: ___ règle.
10. Identify the object: “C'est un cahier.”
11. Complete: Tu ___ un frère ?
12. Write one sentence using Qu'est-ce que c'est ?

### Colours / calendar / Unit 3
13. Choose the correct colour agreement in a simple noun phrase.
14. Put three days of the week in the correct order.
15. Complete a missing month sequence.
16. Match each season with its French expression.
17. Complete: ___ hiver / ___ printemps.
18. Describe one school object using a colour.

### Nationalities / Unit 4
19. Complete: Je ___ égyptien/égyptienne.
20. Choose the correct form for a female French person.
21. Match country and nationality.
22. Ask: “What is your nationality?” in French.
23. Complete the present tense of être for je/tu/il/elle.
24. Transform a masculine nationality statement into feminine form where appropriate.

### Family / Unit 5
25. Choose the correct definite article: ___ frère.
26. Identify a family member from a short description.
27. Complete: C'est ___ sœur.
28. Choose between Qui ? / Comment ? / Quel ? for a given question.
29. Write one sentence introducing a family member.
30. Read a short family profile and extract one relationship.

### Clothing / Unit 6
31. Complete: Je ___ un T-shirt bleu.
32. Choose the correct clothing word for a described item.
33. Ask “What are you wearing?” in French.
34. Describe one item of clothing with a colour.
35. Choose suitable clothing for winter or summer.
36. Read a two-sentence clothing description and identify the item/colour.

## Remediation micro-lesson map
- Items 1–6 → `identity_communication`, `phonology`, `numbers_time`.
- Items 7–12 → `grammar_core`, `classroom`.
- Items 13–18 → `calendar_colours_seasons`.
- Items 19–24 → `nationalities`, `grammar_core`.
- Items 25–30 → `family`, `question_functions`.
- Items 31–36 → `clothing`, `grammar_core`, `reading_production`.

## Final assessment composition — 40 items
- 8 identity/grammar items
- 6 numbers/time items
- 6 classroom/articles items
- 6 colours/calendar/seasons items
- 6 nationalities/être items
- 4 family/question-word items
- 4 clothing/porter items

At least 10 final items must require short production rather than pure recognition.
