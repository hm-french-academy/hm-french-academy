## Coverage policy
The revision pack therefore treats Units 1–6 as mandatory. The current HM Academy Grade 8 material is a useful internal source for the first three areas, but it is not sufficient by itself for the annual bridge.

## External evidence policy

A separately indexed copy of *Le Français Simple 1* exposes the full six-unit sequence and detailed vocabulary/grammar progression. These are used as cross-checks, not as permission to introduce unrelated content.
