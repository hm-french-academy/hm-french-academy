# Grade 8 annual revision — print pack specification v1

## Pack architecture
1. Cover + student information.
2. Diagnostic test (36 items).
3. Unit 1–2 foundation review.
4. Unit 3–4 foundation review.
5. Unit 5–6 foundation review.
6. Mixed annual retrieval drills.
7. Remediation cards by skill domain.
8. Final readiness test (40 items).
9. Answer key + scoring rubric.

## Page design
- A4, printer-friendly, high contrast.
- Short blocks, generous writing space, no dense textbook reproduction.
- French examples are visually distinct from Arabic explanation.
- Every activity has an explicit skill label.
- Use tables only when they improve retrieval or comparison.

## Difficulty progression
- Level 1: recognition and matching.
- Level 2: controlled completion/selection.
- Level 3: transformation and short production.
- Level 4: integrated reading/communication.

## Diagnostic routing
Missed items are tagged by domain. Students should receive only the corresponding remediation cards before retaking a short mastery check.

## Final readiness rule
- Overall readiness: ≥70%.
- Critical-domain mastery: ≥80% preferred.
- Any materially weak critical domain triggers targeted remediation rather than repeating the entire pack.

## Content integrity
Units 4–6 are mandatory because they belong to the full Grade 7 annual course. The pack is not allowed to stop after Unit 3.
