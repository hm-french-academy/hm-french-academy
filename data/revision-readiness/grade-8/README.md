# Grade 8 Revision Readiness — Annual Bridge

## Purpose
Prepare a professional revision pack for Grade 8 (second preparatory) that rebuilds the prerequisite foundation from the **entire Grade 7 academic year**, because the target course is **Le Français Simple 2** and the existing Grade 8 repository source is semester-one oriented.

## Scope rule
The reviewed course is **Le Français Simple 1, Units 1–6, full academic year**. Units 1–3 are represented in the current Grade 8 repository as semester-one-aligned material; Units 4–6 must be restored explicitly from verified annual curriculum evidence and second-semester materials.

## Verified annual sequence
1. Unité 1 — Présente-toi
2. Unité 2 — Tu as quel âge ?
3. Unité 3 — Les couleurs
4. Unité 4 — Les nationalités
5. Unité 5 — La famille
6. Unité 6 — Les vêtements / Qu'est-ce que tu portes ?

The annual distribution source explicitly places Units 1–3 before the first-semester exam and Units 4–6 in February–April, with the end-of-year exam in May. External annual evidence also reproduces the vocabulary/grammar progression for all six units.

## Production standard
The revision is **readiness-based, not a textbook summary**. Every major prerequisite must be represented through:
- concise explanation;
- vocabulary retrieval;
- grammar control;
- pronunciation/reading;
- functional communication;
- graduated practice;
- diagnostic assessment;
- targeted remediation;
- final readiness assessment;
- answer key.

## Source hierarchy
1. Curriculum/annual distribution evidence for scope.
2. Existing HM Academy Grade 8 lesson content where it corresponds to Le Français Simple 1 prerequisites.
3. Verified second-semester Le Français Simple 1 materials.
4. External books such as Original, Bravo and Génial only as supplementary exercise-pattern/reference material.
5. General French resources only for pedagogy, never to silently redefine the curriculum.

## Non-negotiable QA
- No term-one-only revision.
- No invented units or imported unrelated A1 content.
- No duplicate teaching where one concise remediation card is enough.
- Critical prerequisites must be tested in both recognition and production.
- The final pack must be printable and platform-ready.
