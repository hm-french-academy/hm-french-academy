# HM Academy — Grade 9 Annual Revision Print Pack

## Scope
Annual prerequisite review for Grade 9 based on the full Grade 8 learning year, including Semester 1 and Semester 2. This is not a term-1 summary.

## Student pack
1. Cover + student data
2. Diagnostic test
3. Six unit revision modules
4. Grammar quick-reference
5. Vocabulary active-recall sheets
6. Communication tasks
7. Reading tasks
8. Writing tasks
9. Targeted remediation pages
10. Final readiness assessment
11. Student self-evaluation

## Teacher pack
- Answer key
- Diagnostic-to-skill mapping
- Remediation routing table
- Writing rubric
- Speaking/interaction rubric
- Final readiness decision rules

## Print requirements
- A4 portrait
- Ink-efficient layout
- Clear writing spaces
- Student and teacher versions separated
- No copyrighted textbook page reproduction
- Original exercises and examples

## Readiness model
- Strong Ready: >=80% overall and no critical domain below 60%
- Ready: 70–79% overall and no critical domain below 55%
- Targeted Revision: 60–69% or any critical domain below threshold
- Foundation Required: <60%

## Critical domains
- Core verbs: être, avoir, s'appeler, porter
- Articles and gender
- Basic question forms
- Core identity/family/clothing vocabulary
- Basic reading comprehension
- Basic sentence production

## Content integrity
The annual scope must include the second-semester content (nationalities, family, clothing and associated grammar/functions) rather than relying only on the first-semester lesson source currently present in the platform.
