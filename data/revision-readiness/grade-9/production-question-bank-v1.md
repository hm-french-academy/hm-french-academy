# Grade 9 Annual Revision — Production Question Bank v1

## Design principle
The bank is built from the full-year Grade 8 prerequisite scope. Items progress from recognition to recall, controlled production, contextual use, and communication. Second-semester prerequisite domains are mandatory.

## A. Vocabulary recall
1. Write 8 classroom objects in French.
2. Write the four seasons.
3. Write six colours.
4. Write eight family members.
5. Write eight items of clothing.
6. Write six days/months in French.

## B. Core grammar
7. Complete: Je ___ égyptien. (suis / es / est)
8. Complete: Tu ___ onze ans. (ai / as / a)
9. Complete: Elle s'___ Sara. (appelle / appelles / appelons)
10. Complete: Je ___ un pantalon bleu. (porte / portes / portent)
11. Choose: ___ trousse / ___ livre / ___ parents. (un, une, des)
12. Choose: ___ père / ___ mère / ___ oncle / ___ sœurs. (le, la, l', les)
13. Change to feminine: français, italien, allemand, égyptien.
14. Change to plural where appropriate: un livre, une robe, le pantalon.

## C. Communication
15. Ask someone's name and answer.
16. Ask someone's age and answer.
17. Ask someone's nationality and answer.
18. Ask who a person is and answer with a family member.
19. Ask what an object is and identify it.
20. Ask about a colour and answer.
21. Create a 4-line dialogue using name, age and nationality.
22. Create a 4-line dialogue using family and clothing.

## D. Contextual grammar
23. Complete a six-line profile of a student using être, avoir and s'appeler.
24. Complete a clothing description using porter + colours.
25. Correct five deliberate errors in articles and gender.
26. Correct five deliberate errors in être/avoir/s'appeler.

## E. Reading
Text A: « Bonjour ! Je m'appelle Youssef. J'ai douze ans. Je suis égyptien. J'ai un frère et une sœur. Mon frère s'appelle Karim. En hiver, je porte un pull bleu et un pantalon noir. »
27. Quel âge a Youssef ?
28. Quelle est sa nationalité ?
29. Combien de frères/sœurs a-t-il ?
30. Comment s'appelle son frère ?
31. Que porte-t-il en hiver ?
32. Find two examples of core verbs in the text.

## F. Writing
33. Write 6 connected sentences introducing yourself.
34. Write 6 sentences describing your family.
35. Write 5 sentences describing what you wear in one season.
36. Write a short dialogue between two students meeting for the first time.

## G. Integrated performance
37. Read a short student profile and produce a matching oral/written profile without copying the wording.
38. Given five prompts (name, age, nationality, family, clothes), produce a coherent paragraph of 7–8 sentences.
39. Given a family/clothing picture in the printed pack, write 6 observations using articles and core verbs.
40. Pair task: one student asks five identity/family questions; the other answers accurately.

## Scoring architecture
- Items 1–14: foundational accuracy.
- Items 15–22: functional communication.
- Items 23–26: transfer and correction.
- Items 27–32: reading.
- Items 33–36: writing.
- Items 37–40: integrated production.

## Quality gate
No final readiness form may be released until every critical prerequisite appears in at least one closed-book production item and one contextual item. Open-response scoring must use the teacher rubric; acceptable equivalent wording should be allowed where the target form is preserved.
