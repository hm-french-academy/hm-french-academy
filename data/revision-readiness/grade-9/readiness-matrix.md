# Grade 9 French — Annual Prerequisite Readiness Matrix

## Scope correction
This matrix deliberately covers the **full previous academic year** of Le Français Simple 1, not only the currently uploaded first-semester source.

## Semester 1 — core foundations
| Unit | Skill cluster | Must retain |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identity + classroom French | transparent words, alphabet/vowels, greetings, subject pronouns, s'appeler, numbers 0–20, telling the hour |
| 2 | Age + classroom objects | avoir, age questions, un/une/des, school-bag and classroom vocabulary, c'est / qu'est-ce que c'est |
| 3 | Describing basics | colours, days, months, seasons, au/en usage in simple calendar/season expressions |

## Semester 2 — expansion of the foundation
| Unit | Skill cluster | Must retain |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Nationality | être, countries, masculine/feminine nationality forms, asking/giving nationality |
| 5 | Family + questions | family vocabulary, le/la/l'/les, qui, qu'est-ce que, quel/quelle, comment, simple identification |
| 6 | Clothing + description | clothing vocabulary, colours with nouns, porter, seasonal clothing questions and answers |

## High-priority prerequisite links
1. **s'appeler + être + avoir** → basic personal presentation and later descriptive sentences.
2. **Articles + noun gender** → reliable vocabulary production and agreement.
3. **Question words** → classroom interaction and comprehension.
4. **Numbers + age + time** → routine communication.
5. **Colours + clothing** → adjective/noun agreement in simple descriptions.
6. **Family + identity vocabulary** → short reading and writing about people.
7. **Seasons/months/days** → temporal expressions used in later communication.

## Review architecture
- Diagnostic is distributed across all six units; it must not over-sample Unit 1.
- Every weak prerequisite receives a targeted mini-review before the final assessment.
- Practice moves from recognition → controlled production → short communicative production.
- Final assessment samples all six units and prioritizes high-link prerequisites.
- Answer key is separated from student pages.

## External-source policy
Use Bravo, Génial and other general French materials to improve exercise variety, distractor quality, reading micro-texts and communicative practice. Do not import their curriculum sequence or unsupported grammar as if it belonged to Le Français Simple 1.

## Release gate
Do not publish the printable pack as final until the annual map, diagnostic coverage, unit coverage, final assessment coverage and answer key are all cross-checked.
