# Grade 9 Annual Revision — Final Readiness Assessment v1

**Suggested duration:** 40 minutes + optional oral interaction.

## Part A — Core language (12 pts)
1. Je ___ égyptien. (suis / es / est)
2. J'___ onze ans. (ai / as / a)
3. Elle s'___ Lina. (appelle / appelles / appelons)
4. Je ___ un pull bleu. (porte / portes / portent)
5–8. Choose the correct article for: livre, trousse, oncle, parents.
9–10. Convert two nationality adjectives to feminine.
11–12. Write two correct identity questions.

## Part B — Communication (8 pts)
13. Ask and answer about name.
14. Ask and answer about age.
15. Ask and answer about nationality.
16. Ask and answer who a family member is.

## Part C — Reading (10 pts)
Read a short profile combining identity, age, nationality, family and clothing. Answer five explicit questions and identify two core verbs.

## Part D — Writing (10 pts)
Write 7–8 connected sentences about yourself including name, age, nationality, one family member and clothing for a season.

## Part E — Transfer (10 pts)
Given five prompts, produce a coherent mini-dialogue and a short profile without a word bank.

## Scoring
A 40-point written assessment plus a 10-point transfer section = 50 points.

## Decision
80%+ and no critical domain <60% → Strong Ready.
70–79% and no critical domain <55% → Ready.
60–69% or a critical domain below threshold → Targeted Revision.
Below 60% → Foundation Required.
