# HM Academy — Grade 9 Annual Revision Pack

## Before you start
This pack reviews the full previous-year foundation before you begin Grade 9. Work independently during the diagnostic, then complete only the revision modules your teacher assigns.

## Route

4. Nationalities
5. Family
6. Clothes & description
