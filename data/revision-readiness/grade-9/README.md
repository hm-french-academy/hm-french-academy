# HM Academy — Grade 9 French Revision & Readiness

## Purpose
Pre-course revision pack for Grade 9 (2e année préparatoire), reviewing Grade 8 (1re année préparatoire) prerequisites before the current-year course begins.

## Source truth
The Grade 8 learning source currently registered in HM Academy contains 11 lessons across three units: alphabet/transparent words, greetings, subject pronouns and -er verbs, être/presentation, numbers 0–20, time, avoir/age, classroom objects and indefinite articles, colours, definite articles/days, and months/seasons/weather. The source registry explicitly marks the package as the authoritative uploaded source and keeps the Grade 8 template reference-only.

## Pack architecture
1. Diagnostic assessment
2. Unit-by-unit prerequisite review
3. Grammar consolidation
4. Vocabulary active-recall bank
5. Communication and short writing
6. Final readiness assessment
7. Teacher answer key and readiness decision

## Quality rules
- Review only content supported by the Grade 8 source; do not invent prerequisite units.
- Exercises are original practice, not reproduction of textbook pages.
- Separate student-facing material from answer key.
- Keep this layer isolated from live lesson runtime.
- Do not alter Grade 7/Grade 8 lesson content while building the pack.
