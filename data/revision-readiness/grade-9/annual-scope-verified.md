# Grade 9 Readiness — Annual Scope Verification

## Purpose
This file defines the evidence boundary for the revision pack that prepares students for Grade 9.

## Verified in repository
The current Grade 9 curriculum evidence available in the repository is explicitly semester-1 material. Unit 1 QA identifies its source as `3ème prép 1er semestre .pdf` and records source pages 16–38. It covers sports vocabulary, places/characters/verbs, `jouer + à/de`, `faire + de`, second-group verbs, dialogue and exercises.

## Critical correction
The repository evidence currently available is **not sufficient to represent the complete previous academic year**. Therefore the revision pack must NOT be labeled or released as a full-year prerequisite review until semester-2 / second-semester source evidence has been verified.

## External research policy
External web research may be used to discover the missing semester-2 scope and compatible French-learning materials (for example Bravo, Génial and other general French resources), but those materials are secondary references. They must not silently replace the official/actual curriculum.

## Release gate
A full-year Grade 9 readiness pack requires:
1. Verified semester-1 scope.
2. Verified semester-2 scope.
3. Annual skill map combining both semesters.
4. Prerequisite mapping to Grade 9 entry requirements.
5. Diagnostic and remediation coverage for every critical prerequisite.
6. Final readiness assessment.

Until items 1–5 are satisfied, the package remains `RESEARCH_IN_PROGRESS`.
