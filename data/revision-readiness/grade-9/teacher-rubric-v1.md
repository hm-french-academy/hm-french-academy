# Grade 9 Annual Revision — Teacher Rubric v1

## Writing — 10 points
- 4 pts: accurate core structures (être, avoir, s'appeler, porter)
- 2 pts: vocabulary accuracy and relevance
- 2 pts: article/gender agreement control
- 1 pt: coherence and sequencing
- 1 pt: spelling/legibility sufficient for meaning

## Communication — 10 points
- 3 pts: answers the requested function
- 3 pts: correct target forms
- 2 pts: understandable pronunciation/orthography
- 2 pts: interaction completeness (question + answer / turn-taking)

## Reading — 10 points
- 6 pts: explicit information retrieval
- 2 pts: vocabulary in context
- 2 pts: accurate response construction

## Mastery decision
A domain is mastered when the learner demonstrates at least 70% on its checkpoint and succeeds on one transfer item. If the learner repeatedly fails a critical domain, route to Foundation Required regardless of overall score.

## Teacher feedback codes
I = Identity / s'appeler
E = être
A = avoir / âge
G = Gender / articles
Q = Questions / communication
N = Numbers / calendar / time
T = Nationalities
F = Family
C = Clothes / colours / porter
R = Reading
W = Writing

## Feedback rule
Mark the error code first, then prescribe the matching micro-review. Avoid assigning the full pack again unless three or more critical domains remain below threshold.
