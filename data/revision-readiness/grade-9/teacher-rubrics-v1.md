# Grade 9 Annual Revision — Teacher Rubrics v1

## Writing — 8 marks
- 2: task coverage complete (all required information)
- 2: core grammar accuracy (être/avoir/s'appeler/porter)
- 2: vocabulary appropriacy and gender/article control
- 1: sentence connection and clarity
- 1: spelling/legibility

## Communication — 6 marks
- 2: correct question/function
- 2: appropriate response
- 1: core form accuracy
- 1: intelligibility / completion

## Reading — 8 marks
Award 2 marks per explicit-information question. Accept semantically correct answers even with minor spelling errors that do not obscure meaning.

## Remediation policy
Do not penalize a learner twice for the same underlying error within one micro-review. Record the skill code, reteach briefly, then reassess using a fresh item.

## Readiness safeguard
Overall score alone cannot override a critical-domain failure. A learner below the critical-domain threshold is routed to targeted revision even when the total score is high.
