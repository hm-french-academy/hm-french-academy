# Grade 9 Revision — Annual Source Strategy

## Correction to the initial scope
The first implementation draft was incorrectly scoped to the currently available Grade 8 first-semester runtime source. That source is useful for platform alignment, but it is NOT sufficient for an annual revision pack.

## Correct target
The Grade 9 readiness pack must review the learner's **full Grade 8 academic year**, not only semester 1.

## Source hierarchy
1. **Primary:** all available Grade 8 `Le Français Simple 1` material covering both semesters.
2. **Platform source:** HM Academy Grade 8 registry/content, used to preserve terminology and identify what is already implemented.
3. **External annual-source research:** publicly indexed second-semester materials, teacher preparation sheets, revision packs and exams tied specifically to `Le Français Simple 1`.
4. **Supplementary publishers:** Bravo / Original / other French preparatory resources, used for exercise diversity and skill coverage only when compatible with the target curriculum.
5. **General French books:** used only to fill pedagogical practice gaps, never to redefine what the learner was expected to study.

## Evidence found in external research
The publicly indexed Original en français source explicitly contains a listing for **Original Francéais 1re année préparatoire — second semester 2020**, described as a Le Français Simple revision/practice booklet with explanation, exercises and exams. It also separately lists the first-semester booklet and identifies Le Français Simple 1/2/3 as the preparatory-stage series.

## Design consequence
The final pack will NOT be a simple summary of the 11 first-semester lessons. It will be an **Annual Readiness Assessment** with:
- Semester 1 prerequisite map
- Semester 2 prerequisite map
- cumulative vocabulary bank
- cumulative grammar bank
- communication functions
- pronunciation/orthography checkpoints where relevant
- reading comprehension
- guided writing
- cumulative mixed practice
- diagnostic test
- targeted remediation sheets
- final readiness test

## Validation rule
A topic enters the final pack only when it is supported by either:
- the full-year Le Français Simple 1 source itself, or
- at least one curriculum-specific external source plus corroborating evidence.

General French material cannot be promoted to a prerequisite merely because it appears in Bravo/Génial or another unrelated CEFR/general-French book.
