## External evidence

### 1. Original en français — first and second semester materials
Public search results for the Egyptian preparatory curriculum identify separate first- and second-semester preparation material under the Le Français Simple series. The second-semester listing is explicitly titled **Original Francéais 1re année préparatoire ترم ثان** and describes an 80-page booklet for Le Français Simple containing explanations, exercises and exams. The same source also lists the first-semester booklet and a page identifying Le Français Simple 1, 2 and 3 as the government-school preparatory French series.

Source: https://originalenfrancais.blogspot.com/

Searches also surfaced general French methods such as Le Nouveau Taxi, but these are not treated as curriculum truth. They can inform pedagogical exercise design only when a skill is already evidenced in the Egyptian preparatory curriculum.
