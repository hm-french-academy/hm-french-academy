# Grade 9 Annual Revision — Question Blueprint

## Objective
Build a high-quality printable readiness assessment from the full Grade 8 academic year, then route learners to targeted review.

## Distribution
- Vocabulary & lexical retrieval: 25%
- Grammar in context: 25%
- Communication/functions: 15%
- Reading comprehension: 15%
- Guided writing/production: 15%
- Phonology/orthography: 5%

## Cognitive progression
1. Recognition
2. Recall
3. Controlled production
4. Contextual choice
5. Short communicative production

## Required coverage
- Full annual scope of Le Français Simple 1, including second-semester units.
- No semester-one-only bias.
- Every critical prerequisite appears in diagnostic or final assessment.
- High-priority skills appear in at least two different contexts.
- Avoid duplicate questions that test the same surface pattern.

## Diagnostic design
30 items plus one short production task.
Use balanced sampling so a learner cannot pass solely through vocabulary recognition.

## Remediation design
Each missed cluster maps to one micro-review card:
- Identity & greetings
- Alphabet / pronunciation
- Numbers & time
- Être / avoir / s'appeler
- Articles & noun gender
- Classroom vocabulary
- Colours / calendar / seasons
- Nationalities
- Family
- Clothing / porter
- Question words
- Reading & writing

## Final readiness
35 items plus guided writing. Target >=70%; mastery target >=80%. Critical domains must be checked explicitly.

## Content quality
Questions must be original, age-appropriate, unambiguous, and answer-keyable. Open tasks require a scoring rubric rather than a single exact answer.
